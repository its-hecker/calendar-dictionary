# Occasion details in SabCal 2.1

Occasions are keyed to the **adjusted Hijri date**. The grid marker, selected-day banner, monthly list, upcoming-date calculation and detail sheet all use the same lookup. A date may contain more than one event; 28 Safar lists the Prophet's passing and Imam Hasan's commemoration separately.

The missing **13 Jumada al-Awwal** Fatimiyya observance is included and labelled **75-day account**. **3 Jumada al-Thani** is labelled **95-day account**. They are both retained rather than silently choosing a single narration. The birth anniversary on 20 Jumada al-Thani is also listed.

Tapping a date opens its day/occasion sheet. A single occasion opens its detail directly; multiple occasions are listed separately; a date with no listed occasion says so and offers the next listed date. Short profiles are bundled for the named figures. Bibi Fatima's sheet includes birth, passing and resting-place notes, with reported differences made explicit. The app does not claim a settled birth year or precise grave location.

These are short, newly written factual summaries, with links for further reading. They are not complete biographies or a comprehensive religious calendar.
